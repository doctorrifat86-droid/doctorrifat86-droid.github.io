About The ICU Guy – ICU Bundle

ICU Bundle is a free, fast, offline-capable toolkit for intensive-care and acute-medicine clinicians, built by The ICU Guy, a critical-care doctor.

What’s inside

  • 330+ calculators — severity scores (SOFA, APACHE II, SAPS II, NEWS2), sepsis, respiratory and ventilator mechanics, haemodynamics, acid–base, renal and CRRT dosing, neurology, cardiology, haematology, obstetrics, paediatrics, toxicology, anaesthesia and drug-infusion tools.
  • Tap-to-check bundles and diagnostic criteria — sepsis hour-1, VAP and CLABSI prevention, intubation, extubation, CRRT, post-arrest care and many more, with criteria sets that tell you automatically when a definition is met.
  • Patient-case mode — run checklists for a patient with timestamps, stored only on your device.
  • Diseases A–Z linking hundreds of conditions to the relevant tools, plus quick reference charts (vasopressor ranges, ventilator settings, electrolyte replacement, antibiotic durations and more).

Sources

Every tool and table cites the original publication or guideline it is based on (for example Surviving Sepsis Campaign, KDIGO, ATS/ESICM/SCCM, ERC/AHA, IDSA). Content is written independently and summarised for quick bedside use; always check the primary source and local protocols.

Free to use

The app is free and supported by unobtrusive advertising. Ads never appear inside checklists or patient cases.

Install it on your phone: open the app in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and choose Add to Home Screen. It then works offline.
