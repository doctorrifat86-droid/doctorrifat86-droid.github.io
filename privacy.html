Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how The ICU Guy – ICU Bundle (“the app”, “we”) handles information. The app is a free, offline-capable web app for clinicians.

Patient and clinical data stay on your device

Calculator inputs, checklist ticks, patient cases, notes, favourites and recent items are processed entirely in your browser and stored only in your device’s localStorage. We do not have a server that receives this data, and it is never uploaded, sold or shared. It leaves your device only if you choose to share, copy or export a case yourself. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes it. Please avoid entering full patient identifiers; initials or bed numbers are sufficient.

Offline storage

The app uses a service worker and the browser cache to store its own files so it works offline. Advertising scripts are never cached.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

The app may display ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (and similar technologies such as local storage or device identifiers) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads are shown.

Ads are never shown inside checklists or patient-case screens, and ad providers do not receive the clinical information you enter.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP address) for security and operations; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app is intended for health professionals and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
